Get suggested bookmarks as you browse

A small companion browser extension can notice the pages you visit and offer them here as one-click bookmarks — so you stop losing links you meant to save.

It stays entirely on your device: no account, no server, no browsing history kept beyond what's pending. Privacy details.

The extension isn't in a web store yet. You can still add it in about two minutes — load the extension/ folder as an unpacked extension from chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions). Full steps: project README.
